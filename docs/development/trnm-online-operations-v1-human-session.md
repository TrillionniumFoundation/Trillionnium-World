# TRNM Online Operations v1 Human Session

Status: pending independent human participants.

## Required participants

- Two distinct non-developer players using separate CEX accounts and devices.
- One observer who does not coach inputs unless a safety or hard-block issue
  occurs.
- Informed consent for local logs/screenshots; no credential or session-token
  capture.

## 10-15 minute route

1. Player A types a player ID and credential in the native window, saves it to
   the kernel keyring, logs in and connects the cloud character.
2. Player B restarts the launcher, restores an already saved credential and
   connects without a credential environment variable.
3. Both join ranked queue, read the opponent/season state and launch the match.
4. One player closes and reopens the Authority window, reconnects, and both
   finish or reach the agreed session stop.
5. One player opens the authoritative replay and submits a clearly labelled
   test report; the observer confirms that no secret is visible.

## Evidence that must be human-authored

- Participant aliases and consent timestamps.
- Start/end timestamps and duration.
- At least three confusion or hesitation observations with screen/state and
  elapsed time.
- Each player's 1-5 ratings for login clarity, queue clarity, battle control
  and reconnect confidence.
- Observer verdict, top blocker and a link to redacted screenshots/video if
  consented.

Automation, scripted X11 input, developer self-observation and prefilled
templates receive zero human-session credit.
